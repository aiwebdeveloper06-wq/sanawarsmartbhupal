WELCOME TO SANAWAR SMART SCHOOL

Nurturing Wholesome Personalities for the 21st Century

"Let Your Light Shine"

Welcome to the official website of Sanawar Smart School Bhupal (Mansa). Our foundational commitment is to provide a stress-free environment for children to make them intellectually, emotionally, and psychologically strong individuals.

In the 21st century, we need a system of education that promises a wholesome personality for each learner. The Sanawar Smart School Bhupal (Mansa) family strives at its best level to develop the learning abilities of its students and provides experiences that enable them to explore the outside world and find meaningful connections between the world around them and their inner selves.

Every child is differently able, and if one has not yet excelled in a particular area, it is only because their potential is waiting to be realized. The seeds of lifelong success are sown in the early years of a child's life.

MESSAGE FROM THE PRINCIPAL

Guiding Young Minds Toward Academic & Moral Distinction

"At Sanawar Smart School Bhupal, our endeavour is to nurture intellectually capable, emotionally balanced, and socially responsible citizens in a stress-free learning environment where every child's unique talent is discovered and honoured."

LEADERSHIP, CULTURE & CREATIVITY

Four-House System & Co-Curricular Life

Sanawar Smart School Bhupal places deep emphasis on forming confident leaders. Every year, democratic elections are organized to constitute the Student Council, and the entire student body is organized into four distinguished Houses:

HOUSE I
Mother Teresa House
HOUSE II
Bhagat Singh House
HOUSE III
Dr. Kalam House
HOUSE IV
Ms. Kalpana House

Regular inter-house competitions include Song, Dance, Art & Craft, Science Quiz, Mathematics Quiz, Mehandi, Diya Making, Fancy Dress, Calligraphy, Rangoli, Spelling Contests, and Best Out of Waste, alongside educational tours and seasonal school festivals.

ATHLETICS, FITNESS & INDIGENOUS GAMES

Annual Sports Meet & Physical Education

Every year, the Annual Sports Meet is held on the school campus with a colourful opening ceremony. Respected dignitaries are invited as the Chief Guest to declare the meet open, followed by a skilled march past of house athletes.

Our young athletes demonstrate their talent in track and field events including heats, relay, and long jump, with medals and trophies presented to winners by our Chairman. Regular classes in Yoga, Gymnastics, Skating, and Karate nurture physical fitness, while indigenous sports like Kho-Kho and Kabaddi are a proud routine part of our school and have won prizes at various levels.
